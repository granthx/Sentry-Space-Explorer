Mars Terrain Segmentation

A segmentation model labels every pixel of Curiosity's camera images as soil, bedrock, sand or big rock — so a rover knows where it can drive safely. NASA's Spirit rover got stuck in soft sand in 2009 and never moved again.

About AI4Mars

The model was trained on the AI4Mars dataset published by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Thousands of volunteers labelled Curiosity rover images on Zooniverse, assigning every pixel to one of four terrain classes. The resulting dataset is used by NASA to train autonomous driving algorithms for future rovers.
